# NCT03914612: Testing the Addition of the Immunotherapy Drug Pembrolizumab to the Usual Chemotherapy Treatment (Paclitaxel and Carboplatin) in Stage III-IV or Recurrent Endometrial Cancer

Source: ClinicalTrials.gov posted results, NCT03914612 (https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03914612).

Phase: PHASE3. Enrollment: 813.

## Baseline participants

- (dMMR Cohort): Arm 1: Active Comparator (Placebo, Paclitaxel, Carboplatin): 113 participants
- (dMMR Cohort): Arm 2: Experimental (Pembrolizumab, Paclitaxel, Carboplatin): 112 participants
- (pMMR Cohort): Arm 1: Active Comparator (Placebo, Paclitaxel, Carboplatin): 295 participants
- (pMMR Cohort): Arm 2: Experimental (Pembrolizumab, Paclitaxel, Carboplatin): 293 participants
- Total: 813 participants

## Outcome (primary): Progression-free Survival (PFS)

Time frame: Duration of time from study entry to time of progression per RECIST criteria or death due to any cause, whichever occurs first. The interquartile range for follow-up was (6.37 months, 30.62 months). The maximum for follow-up was 37.85 months.
Unit: Months; measure: MEDIAN; dispersion: 95% Confidence Interval

Population: Randomized population (with determinate MMR status)

- (dMMR Cohort): Arm 1: Active Comparator (Placebo, Paclitaxel, Carboplatin): 7.6 (6.4 to 9.9)
- (dMMR Cohort): Arm 2: Experimental (Pembrolizumab, Paclitaxel, Carboplatin): NA (30.6 to NA)
- (pMMR Cohort): Arm 1: Active Comparator (Placebo, Paclitaxel, Carboplatin): 8.7 (8.4 to 10.7)
- (pMMR Cohort): Arm 2: Experimental (Pembrolizumab, Paclitaxel, Carboplatin): 11.7 (11.0 to 15.0)
