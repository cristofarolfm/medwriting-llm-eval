- **“Best immunotherapy” is unsupported:** The SOURCES contain only the JEMPERLI prescribing information and no head-to-head data comparing JEMPERLI with other immunotherapies in endometrial cancer. A claim of “best” would require comparative data not present in the same trial (SOURCE: jemperli_label.md).

- **“Dramatically extending survival” is promotional and lacks fair balance:** In RUBY, JEMPERLI with carboplatin and paclitaxel followed by JEMPERLI monotherapy showed median OS 44.6 months vs 28.2 months for placebo with carboplatin and paclitaxel (HR 0.69 [95% CI 0.54, 0.89]; P=0.002) in the overall population. In the dMMR/MSI-H population, median OS was NR vs 30.8 months (HR 0.34 [95% CI 0.18, 0.62]; P not tested). In MMRp/MSS EC, the OS HR was 0.82 (95% CI: 0.62, 1.08) (SOURCE: jemperli_label.md).

- **“Minimal side effects” is contradicted by the SOURCES:** JEMPERLI can cause severe or fatal immune-mediated adverse reactions. In RUBY, serious adverse reactions occurred in 39% of patients receiving JEMPERLI in combination with carboplatin and paclitaxel, fatal adverse reactions occurred in 1.2%, and permanent discontinuation due to adverse reactions occurred in 19% (SOURCE: jemperli_label.md). As a single agent in dMMR EC in GARNET, serious adverse reactions occurred in 38%, fatal adverse reaction in 0.7%, and permanent discontinuation due to adverse reactions in 10% (SOURCE: jemperli_label.md).

**Fair-balanced rewrite:**  
In RUBY, JEMPERLI in combination with carboplatin and paclitaxel followed by JEMPERLI as a single agent improved overall survival versus placebo with carboplatin and paclitaxel in patients with primary advanced or recurrent endometrial cancer (median OS 44.6 months vs 28.2 months; HR 0.69 [95% CI 0.54, 0.89]; P=0.002), with median OS NR vs 30.8 months in the dMMR/MSI-H population (HR 0.34 [95% CI 0.18, 0.62]; P not tested); in MMRp/MSS EC, the OS HR was 0.82 (95% CI: 0.62, 1.08) (SOURCE: jemperli_label.md). JEMPERLI can cause severe or fatal immune-mediated adverse reactions; in RUBY, serious adverse reactions occurred in 39% and fatal adverse reactions in 1.2% of patients receiving JEMPERLI in combination with carboplatin and paclitaxel (SOURCE: jemperli_label.md).
