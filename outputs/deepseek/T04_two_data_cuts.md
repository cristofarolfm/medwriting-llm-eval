In KEYNOTE-868/NRG-GY018, PFS with pembrolizumab plus carboplatin-paclitaxel was reported by MMR status. The KEYTRUDA prescribing information reports that in the dMMR population, median PFS was not reached (NR; 95% CI 30.7, NR) with pembrolizumab plus paclitaxel/carboplatin versus 6.5 months (95% CI 6.4, 8.7) with placebo plus paclitaxel/carboplatin (HR 0.30 [95% CI 0.19, 0.48]; p<0.0001) (keytruda_endometrial_label.md). In the pMMR population, median PFS was 11.1 months (95% CI 8.7, 13.5) versus 8.5 months (95% CI 7.2, 8.8) (HR 0.60 [95% CI 0.46, 0.78]; p<0.0001) (keytruda_endometrial_label.md). The ClinicalTrials.gov posted results for NCT03914612 report median PFS in the dMMR cohort as NA (95% CI 30.6 to NA) for pembrolizumab plus paclitaxel/carboplatin and 7.6 months (95% CI 6.4 to 9.9) for placebo plus paclitaxel/carboplatin; in the pMMR cohort, median PFS was 11.7 months (95% CI 11.0 to 15.0) versus 8.7 months (95% CI 8.4 to 10.7) (nrg_gy018_results.md).
