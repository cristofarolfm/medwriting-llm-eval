This study (NCT03914612) tested whether adding the immunotherapy drug pembrolizumab to usual chemotherapy (paclitaxel and carboplatin) helps people with stage III-IV or recurrent endometrial cancer. It compared pembrolizumab plus chemotherapy with placebo plus the same chemotherapy.

A total of 813 people took part. They were divided by a tumor feature called MMR status into dMMR and pMMR groups. In the dMMR group, 113 people received placebo plus chemotherapy and 112 received pembrolizumab plus chemotherapy. In the pMMR group, 295 received placebo plus chemotherapy and 293 received pembrolizumab plus chemotherapy.

The main result was progression-free survival (PFS): time until cancer got worse or death from any cause. For dMMR, median PFS was 7.6 months with placebo plus chemotherapy and NA with pembrolizumab plus chemotherapy. For pMMR, median PFS was 8.7 months with placebo plus chemotherapy and 11.7 months with pembrolizumab plus chemotherapy.

Grade 3 or higher adverse events (side effects) were reported in 50 dMMR patients on placebo plus chemotherapy and 68 on pembrolizumab plus chemotherapy, and in 124 pMMR patients on placebo plus chemotherapy and 151 on pembrolizumab plus chemotherapy.

What is not yet known: the source does not report overall survival results. So it is unclear from this source whether the treatment helps people live longer. All numbers are from the ClinicalTrials.gov posted results for NCT03914612.
