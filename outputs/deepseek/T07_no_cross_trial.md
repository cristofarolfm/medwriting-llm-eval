The SOURCES do not contain a head-to-head trial of dostarlimab versus pembrolizumab with chemotherapy in dMMR primary advanced endometrial cancer, so direct comparative numbers are not available. I cannot say which is better from these SOURCES.

JEMPERLI label (RUBY): in the dMMR/MSI-H population, dostarlimab with carboplatin and paclitaxel versus placebo with carboplatin and paclitaxel: OS HR 0.34 (95% CI 0.18, 0.62); PFS HR 0.29 (95% CI 0.17, 0.50), p<0.0001; ORR 74% (95% CI 58, 86) versus 62% (95% CI 47, 76); complete response 26% versus 11%; median DOR NR (range 3.4, 28.3+) versus 5.4 months (range 2.7, 27.2+).

KEYTRUDA label (KEYNOTE-868): in the dMMR population, pembrolizumab with paclitaxel and carboplatin versus placebo with paclitaxel and carboplatin: PFS HR 0.30 (95% CI 0.19, 0.48), p<0.0001; median PFS NR (95% CI 30.7, NR) versus 6.5 months (95% CI 6.4, 8.7). At the PFS analysis, OS data were not mature, with 12% deaths in the dMMR population.

These are separate trials; no head-to-head conclusion is supported.
