- Correction: Median overall survival in the overall population was 44.6 (32.6, NR) months for JEMPERLI with carboplatin and paclitaxel versus 28.2 (22.1, 35.6) months for placebo with carboplatin and paclitaxel (95% CI).
- Source: `jemperli_label.md`, Section 14.1, Table 9, Efficacy Results of Endometrial Cancer Population in RUBY, Overall Survival (OS).

- Source: `jemperli_label.md`, Section 14.1, Table 9, Progression-Free Survival (PFS), dMMR/MSI-H Population.

- Quoted text: “overall survival was also statistically significantly improved (HR 0.34)”

- Source: `jemperli_label.md`, Section 14.1, Table 9, Overall Survival (OS), and accompanying efficacy discussion.

- Source: `jemperli_label.md`, Section 1.1, Indications and Usage – Endometrial Cancer.
