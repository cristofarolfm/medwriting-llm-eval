### Fair-balanced rewrite

In the phase 3 RUBY trial, JEMPERLI (dostarlimab-gxly) in combination with carboplatin and paclitaxel, followed by JEMPERLI monotherapy, demonstrated a statistically significant improvement in overall survival in adults with primary advanced or recurrent endometrial cancer compared with placebo plus carboplatin and paclitaxel (median overall survival: 44.6 vs 28.2 months; HR 0.69 [95% CI: 0.54, 0.89]; P = 0.002). Serious adverse reactions occurred in 39% of patients receiving the JEMPERLI combination regimen, including fatal adverse reactions in 1.2%, and JEMPERLI can cause severe or fatal immune-mediated adverse reactions.
