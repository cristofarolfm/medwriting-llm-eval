## Indication

Lenvatinib (LENVIMA) in combination with pembrolizumab (KEYTRUDA) is indicated for patients with advanced endometrial carcinoma that is mismatch repair proficient (pMMR) or not microsatellite instability-high (MSI-H), as determined by an FDA-approved or FDA-authorized test, who have disease progression following prior systemic therapy in any setting and are not candidates for curative surgery or radiation. The patient described may meet these criteria, subject to confirmation of eligibility. (Sources: LENVIMA prescribing information, Section 1.4; KEYTRUDA prescribing information, Section 1.17.)&#x20;

## Efficacy

In the randomized, open-label Phase 3 Study 309/KEYNOTE-775, lenvatinib plus pembrolizumab was compared with investigator's choice of doxorubicin or paclitaxel in previously platinum-treated advanced endometrial carcinoma.

Among patients with pMMR or not MSI-H disease (n=697), results were:

| Endpoint                         | Lenvatinib + pembrolizumab (n=346) | Chemotherapy (n=351) |
| -------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| Median overall survival          | 17.4 months                        | 12.0 months          |
| OS hazard ratio (95% CI)         | 0.68 (0.56, 0.84)                  | —                    |
| Median progression-free survival | 6.6 months                         | 3.8 months           |
| PFS hazard ratio (95% CI)        | 0.60 (0.50, 0.72)                  | —                    |
| Objective response rate          | 30%                                | 15%                  |
| Median duration of response      | 9.2 months                         | 5.7 months           |

OS (p=0.0001), PFS (p<0.0001), and ORR (p<0.0001) favored combination therapy. These findings establish efficacy relative to the trial's chemotherapy comparators, not other treatment regimens. (Sources: LENVIMA Section 14.4; KEYTRUDA Section 14.17.)&#x20;

## Dosing

The recommended regimen is lenvatinib 20 mg orally once daily plus pembrolizumab 200 mg intravenously over 30 minutes every 3 weeks, until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity.

For lenvatinib-related adverse reactions, sequential dose reductions are 14 mg, 10 mg, and 8 mg once daily. Severe renal or hepatic impairment requires a starting lenvatinib dose of 10 mg once daily. (Source: LENVIMA Section 2.)&#x20;

## Key safety considerations

Lenvatinib requires proactive toxicity monitoring and management:

- Hypertension: Control before treatment; monitor after 1 week, every 2 weeks for the first 2 months, then at least monthly.
- Hepatotoxicity: Monitor liver function before treatment, every 2 weeks for the first 2 months, then monthly.
- Renal impairment/proteinuria: Monitor proteinuria and promptly manage diarrhea or dehydration.
- Additional serious risks: Cardiac dysfunction, arterial thromboembolism, gastrointestinal perforation or fistula, QT prolongation, hemorrhage, and impaired wound healing.

Treatment interruption, dose reduction, or permanent discontinuation may be necessary depending on severity. Pembrolizumab-specific modifications should follow its prescribing information. (Source: LENVIMA Sections 2.7 and 5.)&#x20;
