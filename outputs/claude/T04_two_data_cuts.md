**Speaker note: KEYNOTE-868/NRG-GY018, PFS by MMR status**

In KEYNOTE-868/NRG-GY018, adding pembrolizumab to carboplatin-paclitaxel improved investigator-assessed PFS compared with placebo plus chemotherapy in both MMR cohorts. The KEYTRUDA prescribing information reports an interim PFS analysis, with information fractions of 49% for dMMR and 55% for pMMR. In the dMMR population, median PFS was NR (30.7, NR) with pembrolizumab (n=110) versus 6.5 (6.4, 8.7) months with placebo (n=112), HR 0.30 (0.19, 0.48), p<0.0001. In the pMMR population, median PFS was 11.1 (8.7, 13.5) versus 8.5 (7.2, 8.8) months (n=294 per arm), HR 0.60 (0.46, 0.78), p<0.0001. The ClinicalTrials.gov posted results (NCT03914612) report different medians. In dMMR, they were NA (30.6 to NA) versus 7.6 (6.4 to 9.9) months, and in pMMR, 11.7 (11.0 to 15.0) versus 8.7 (8.4 to 10.7) months, with maximum follow-up of 37.85 months. No hazard ratios are posted there. Neither source explains why the values differ, for example a different data cutoff or analysis population. The two sets should therefore be presented separately rather than combined. Per the prescribing information, OS was not mature at the PFS analysis.
