**No head-to-head data are available in the SOURCES.** Dostarlimab and pembrolizumab were not compared in the same trial, so these labels cannot show which is better. Each was tested only against placebo plus carboplatin and paclitaxel, in separate trials. The results below belong to each trial alone and should not be compared with each other.

**Dostarlimab: RUBY (jemperli_label.md), dMMR/MSI-H population (N = 60 vs N = 62)**
- PFS: median 30.3 months (95% CI 11.8, NR) vs 7.7 months (5.6, 9.7); HR 0.29 (0.17, 0.50); P <0.0001
- OS: median NR (NR, NR) vs 30.8 months (18.7, NR); HR 0.34 (0.18, 0.62); P-value "Not tested"
- ORR: 74% (58, 86) vs 62% (47, 76)

**Pembrolizumab: KEYNOTE-868/NRG-GY018 (keytruda_endometrial_label.md), dMMR population (n = 110 vs n = 112)**
- PFS (interim analysis, information fraction 49%): median NR (30.7, NR) vs 6.5 months (6.4, 8.7); HR 0.30 (0.19, 0.48); p <0.0001
- OS: not mature at the PFS analysis (12% deaths in the dMMR population). No OS estimates are reported.

**Why the two trials can't be compared:**
- **Eligibility:** RUBY enrolled patients with carcinosarcoma. KEYNOTE-868 excluded endometrial sarcoma, including carcinosarcoma.
- **Treatment length:** RUBY allowed up to 3 years. KEYNOTE-868 allowed a maximum of 20 cycles (up to approximately 24 months).
- **Tumor assessment:** schedules differed between the trials.
- **Analysis stage:** the KEYNOTE-868 PFS results come from an interim analysis.
- **Populations:** RUBY reports a "dMMR/MSI-H" population, while KEYNOTE-868 reports a "dMMR cohort."

The SOURCES contain no data that would let anyone judge the relative efficacy or safety of the two regimens.
