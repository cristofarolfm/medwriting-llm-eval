**Summary**

In RUBY, a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial, prespecified exploratory analyses of PFS and OS were performed in patients with MMRp/MSS endometrial cancer (EC). These analyses were exploratory and no P-values are reported for them, so no conclusions about efficacy in this subgroup should be drawn from them. The source does not report ORR, duration of response, or safety data separately for the MMRp/MSS subgroup. [jemperli_label.md]

**Labeled indication**

JEMPERLI, in combination with carboplatin and paclitaxel and followed by JEMPERLI as a single agent, is indicated for adult patients with primary advanced or recurrent EC. The indication is not restricted by MMR status. The recommended dosage is 500 mg every 3 weeks for 6 cycles with carboplatin and paclitaxel. This is followed by 1,000 mg monotherapy every 6 weeks until disease progression, unacceptable toxicity, or up to 3 years. [jemperli_label.md]

**Efficacy in the MMRp/MSS subgroup**

RUBY enrolled 494 patients, of whom 76% had MMRp/MSS tumors. In the overall population, the study showed statistically significant improvements in OS (HR 0.69; 95% CI: 0.54, 0.89) and PFS (HR 0.64; 95% CI: 0.51, 0.80). [jemperli_label.md]

The exploratory results in MMRp/MSS patients were as follows [jemperli_label.md]:

- **OS (n = 372):** HR 0.82 (95% CI: 0.62, 1.08). Median OS was 32.5 months (95% CI: 28.6, NR) with JEMPERLI plus chemotherapy versus 28.2 months (95% CI: 21.9, 36.1) with placebo plus chemotherapy.
- **PFS:** HR 0.78 (95% CI: 0.60, 1.00). Median PFS was 9.8 months (95% CI: 9.0, 12.6) with JEMPERLI plus chemotherapy (n = 185) versus 7.9 months (95% CI: 7.6, 9.8) with placebo plus chemotherapy (n = 187).

**Safety**

The safety data below come from the RUBY safety population as a whole (JEMPERLI arm n = 241). They are not reported by MMR status. [jemperli_label.md]

- Serious adverse reactions occurred in 39% of patients.
- Fatal adverse reactions occurred in 1.2%, including septic shock (0.8%) and myelosuppression (0.4%).
- JEMPERLI was permanently discontinued due to adverse reactions in 46 patients (19%), and dosage was interrupted in 37%.
- Hypothyroidism occurred in 12% (30/241).
- Rash of any grade occurred in 37% with JEMPERLI versus 18% with placebo.

Warnings and precautions include severe or fatal immune-mediated adverse reactions, infusion-related reactions, complications of allogeneic HSCT, and embryo-fetal toxicity. There are no contraindications. [jemperli_label.md]
