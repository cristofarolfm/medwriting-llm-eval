**Why the proposed sentence is not acceptable**

- **"The best immunotherapy" is an unsupported comparison.** The SOURCES contain no trial comparing JEMPERLI with any other immunotherapy. RUBY compared JEMPERLI plus carboplatin and paclitaxel with *placebo* plus carboplatin and paclitaxel. GARNET was a single-arm, open-label trial. The label also states that "adverse reaction rates observed in the clinical trials of a drug cannot be directly compared with rates in the clinical trials of another drug" (jemperli_label.md, Section 6.1).

- **"Dramatically extending survival" overstates the evidence and ignores its context.**
  - The overall survival benefit was shown for JEMPERLI *in combination with carboplatin and paclitaxel* in the RUBY overall population: median 44.6 vs 28.2 months, HR 0.69 (95% CI 0.54, 0.89) (jemperli_label.md, Table 9). It was not shown for JEMPERLI alone.
  - In the dMMR/MSI-H population, the OS P-value was "Not tested" (Table 9).
  - In the exploratory MMRp/MSS analysis, the OS HR was 0.82 (95% CI: 0.62, 1.08) (Section 14.1).
  - The single-agent endometrial data from GARNET report ORR and DOR, not survival (Table 10).
  - "Dramatically" is promotional language.

- **"Minimal side effects" is contradicted by the label.** With the JEMPERLI combination in RUBY, serious adverse reactions occurred in 39% of patients and fatal adverse reactions in 1.2%. JEMPERLI was permanently discontinued due to adverse reactions in 46 patients (19%) (jemperli_label.md, Section 6.1). The label also warns that immune-mediated adverse reactions "may be severe or fatal" and "can occur in any organ system or tissue" (Section 5).

**Fair-balanced rewrite**

"In the randomized, double-blind RUBY trial in adults with primary advanced or recurrent endometrial cancer, JEMPERLI in combination with carboplatin and paclitaxel, followed by JEMPERLI as a single agent, improved overall survival compared with placebo plus carboplatin and paclitaxel in the overall population (median 44.6 vs 28.2 months; HR 0.69 [95% CI 0.54, 0.89]). In this trial, serious adverse reactions occurred in 39% and fatal adverse reactions in 1.2% of patients receiving the JEMPERLI combination, and JEMPERLI can cause immune-mediated adverse reactions, which may be severe or fatal, in any organ system or tissue."

All figures are from jemperli_label.md: Table 9 for efficacy, Section 6.1 for adverse reaction rates, and Section 5 for the immune-mediated warning.
