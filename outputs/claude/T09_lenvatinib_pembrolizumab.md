**Indication**

Lenvatinib (LENVIMA) with pembrolizumab is indicated for advanced endometrial carcinoma that is pMMR or not MSI-H, as determined by an FDA-approved test. Patients must have disease progression following prior systemic therapy in any setting and must not be candidates for curative surgery or radiation [1, 2]. Select patients based on MMR or MSI status in tumor specimens [1].

**Efficacy**

The evidence comes from Study 309/KEYNOTE-775 (NCT03517449), an open-label, randomized, active-controlled trial. It enrolled 827 patients who had received at least one prior platinum-based chemotherapy regimen [1, 2]. Among the 697 pMMR or not MSI-H patients, 346 received lenvatinib plus pembrolizumab and 351 received investigator's choice of doxorubicin (n=254) or paclitaxel (n=97). Results, combination versus control [1, 2]:

- **Overall survival:** median 17.4 (14.2, 19.9) vs 12.0 (10.8, 13.3) months; HR 0.68 (0.56, 0.84); p=0.0001
- **Progression-free survival (BICR):** median 6.6 (5.6, 7.4) vs 3.8 (3.6, 5.0) months; HR 0.60 (0.50, 0.72); p<0.0001
- **Objective response rate:** 30% (26, 36) vs 15% (12, 19), including complete response 5% vs 3%
- **Median duration of response:** 9.2 (1.6+, 23.7+) vs 5.7 (0.0+, 24.2+) months

Patients with carcinosarcoma, active autoimmune disease, or a condition requiring immunosuppression were ineligible [1, 2].

**Dosing**

Give lenvatinib 20 mg orally once daily with pembrolizumab 200 mg IV over 30 minutes every 3 weeks, until unacceptable toxicity or disease progression [1]. In the trial, pembrolizumab was given for a maximum of 24 months [1, 2].

- **Dose reductions for adverse reactions:** 14 mg, then 10 mg, then 8 mg once daily [1].
- **Severe renal impairment** (creatinine clearance less than 30 mL/min) **or severe hepatic impairment** (Child-Pugh C): 10 mg once daily [1].
- **Managing toxicity:** modify one or both drugs as appropriate, and refer to the pembrolizumab prescribing information [1].

**Key safety considerations**

The SOURCES do not include adverse-reaction data from Study 309/KEYNOTE-775 or pembrolizumab's warnings, so consult the full prescribing information for both.

Lenvatinib warnings include [1]:
- hypertension (control blood pressure before treatment and monitor during it)
- cardiac dysfunction
- arterial thromboembolic events (5% with lenvatinib plus pembrolizumab in CLEAR)
- hepatotoxicity
- renal failure
- proteinuria
- diarrhea
- fistula or gastrointestinal perforation
- QT prolongation
- hypocalcemia
- RPLS
- hemorrhage
- thyroid dysfunction (monitor monthly)
- impaired wound healing (withhold at least 1 week before elective surgery)
- osteonecrosis of the jaw
- embryo-fetal toxicity (contraception for 30 days after the last dose)

Most of the reported frequencies come from trials in other tumor types and are not specific to endometrial carcinoma.
