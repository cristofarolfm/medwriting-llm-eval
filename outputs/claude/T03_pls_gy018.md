# Plain-Language Summary: The NRG-GY018 Study

**What was tested?**
This study included people with endometrial cancer that was advanced (stage III–IV) or had come back. Everyone got the usual chemotherapy, paclitaxel and carboplatin. About half also got pembrolizumab, an immunotherapy drug. The other half got a placebo (a dummy treatment) instead.

**Who took part?**
In total, 813 people took part. They were put into two groups based on a tumor test called "MMR status": dMMR and pMMR. The study results do not explain what this test measures.

- dMMR group: 112 people got pembrolizumab and 113 got placebo.
- pMMR group: 293 people got pembrolizumab and 295 got placebo.

**What was found?**
The main question was how long people lived before their cancer grew or they died. Results are given as the "middle" (median) time.

- In the dMMR group, the middle time was 7.6 months with placebo. For the pembrolizumab group, no middle time was reported. It was listed as "NA."
- In the pMMR group, the middle time was 8.7 months with placebo and 11.7 months with pembrolizumab.

Serious side effects happened in more people who got pembrolizumab:

- dMMR group: 66 of 109 people with pembrolizumab, compared with 50 of 106 with placebo.
- pMMR group: 152 of 276 people with pembrolizumab, compared with 125 of 274 with placebo.

**What is not yet known?**
- Whether pembrolizumab helps people live longer overall. These results have not been posted yet.
- How certain these differences are. The results do not include statistical tests.

*All numbers come from the ClinicalTrials.gov results for NCT03914612 (source: nrg_gy018_results.md).*
